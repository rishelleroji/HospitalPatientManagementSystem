How it works

Three steps from waiting room to treatment

01

Register

Enter a patient's ID, name, age and illness. Emergency patients also get a severity: Critical, Serious or Moderate.

02

Queue

Regular patients join the back of a Queue. Emergency patients are placed by a PriorityQueue using a custom comparator.

03

Call

The doctor calls the front of the regular queue, or the most urgent emergency, and the patient leaves the queue.

The queues

Two structures, two rules

Queue (FIFO)

Uses add() at the rear and poll() from the front, so the patient who arrived first is seen first.

PriorityQueue

Smaller priority number is more urgent: Critical 1, Serious 2, Moderate 3. If severity is equal, the earlier arrival goes first.

The heap inside a PriorityQueue is only partly sorted, so only the next patient is guaranteed to be correct. The dashboard shows both views.
